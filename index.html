01 / About me

Product direction meets technical depth.

I work at the intersection of cloud technology, product management, and user experience.

My role involves understanding how cloud services work technically, then translating that understanding into functional requirements, acceptance criteria, roadmaps, and platform features that people can use.

I collaborate with engineering, operations, security, UX/UI, and other cross-functional teams to move cloud-product ideas from requirements to usable capabilities.
